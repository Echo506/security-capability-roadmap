# 12 to 18 Month Roadmap

## Horizon 1: 0 to 3 months
- Define capability baseline.
- Confirm owners and dependencies.
- Prioritize top risks.

## Horizon 2: 3 to 9 months
- Improve IAM controls.
- Expand monitoring coverage.
- Standardize vulnerability workflows.

## Horizon 3: 9 to 18 months
- Mature cloud security governance.
- Improve metrics and executive reporting.
- Consolidate overlapping controls or tools.
