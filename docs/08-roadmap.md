# 12 to 18 Month Roadmap

## Horizon 1: 0 to 3 months

- Confirm current-state capability baseline.
- Assign named owners for each strategic capability.
- Prioritize top identity, cloud, and detection gaps.
- Define KPIs for roadmap tracking.

## Horizon 2: 3 to 9 months

- Expand MFA and formalize access review cycles.
- Launch a cloud security posture management pilot.
- Improve SIEM use cases for phishing, ransomware, and credential misuse.
- Standardize vulnerability remediation reporting.

## Horizon 3: 9 to 18 months

- Mature cloud governance and guardrail coverage.
- Improve executive reporting for capability maturity and risk reduction.
- Consolidate overlapping controls and streamline ownership.
- Establish repeatable operating-model reviews across teams.
