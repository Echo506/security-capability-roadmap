# Project Charter

## Company Profile

- **Company name:** NovaCommerce Group
- **Industry:** E-commerce and digital marketing services
- **Geographic scope:** North America, Latin America, and Europe
- **Employees:** 4,500
- **Infrastructure:** Hybrid environment with on-premises systems and AWS/Azure workloads
- **Regulatory drivers:** GDPR, privacy obligations, contractual security requirements, and ISO 27001 alignment
- **Threat profile:** Phishing, credential theft, ransomware, cloud misconfiguration, exposed APIs, and third-party risk

## Business Context

NovaCommerce Group operates customer-facing web platforms, internal business systems, marketing-data platforms, and cloud-hosted applications. The company is expanding cloud services quickly, but security capabilities have evolved unevenly across regions and teams.

## Security Objectives

- Reduce identity-related risk through stronger MFA, privileged-access controls, and access reviews.
- Improve cloud visibility and misconfiguration detection.
- Strengthen incident detection, escalation, and response coordination.
- Establish consistent ownership, reporting, and security capability governance.
