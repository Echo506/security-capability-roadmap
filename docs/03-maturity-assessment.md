# Maturity Assessment

Use a simple scale:

- 0 = Not established
- 1 = Ad hoc
- 2 = Developing
- 3 = Defined
- 4 = Managed
- 5 = Optimized

| Capability | Score | Evidence | Risks | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IAM | 2 | MFA partial, no full review cycle | Account compromise | 4 |
| Vulnerability Management | 2 | Scanning exists, patching inconsistent | Exposure window | 4 |
