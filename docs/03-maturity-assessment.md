# Maturity Assessment

Use the following scale:

- 0 = Not established
- 1 = Ad hoc
- 2 = Developing
- 3 = Defined
- 4 = Managed
- 5 = Optimized

| Capability | Current Score | Evidence | Primary Risk | Target Score |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Identity and Access Management | 2 | MFA is not enforced for all users; access reviews are inconsistent | Account takeover and excessive access | 4 |
| Cloud Security | 1 | Cloud accounts have inconsistent tagging and no centralized CSPM capability | Misconfiguration and exposed data | 4 |
| SIEM and Detection | 2 | Logs exist but priority detection use cases are incomplete | Delayed detection of malicious activity | 4 |
| Incident Response | 3 | Response process exists, but metrics and regional coordination vary | Inconsistent containment and recovery | 4 |
| Vulnerability Management | 2 | Scanning occurs, but remediation SLAs are not consistently tracked | Prolonged exploitable exposure | 4 |
| Data Protection | 2 | Encryption practices exist but classification is incomplete | Improper handling of sensitive data | 4 |
| GRC and Risk Management | 2 | Risk assessments occur, but evidence and ownership are fragmented | Weak audit readiness and unclear accountability | 4 |
